# MarketScope Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 13, 2026

## 1. About MarketScope

MarketScope is a desktop application designed for eBay marketplace research, listing analysis, seller analytics, and sales intelligence.

MarketScope uses authorized eBay APIs to retrieve information necessary to provide these features.

## 2. Information We Access

With the account owner's authorization, MarketScope may access:

- eBay listing identifiers and inventory information.
- Listing traffic and performance metrics.
- Information about eligibility for seller-initiated offers.
- Public eBay marketplace information used for research and analysis.

MarketScope does not request or store the user's eBay account password.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information obtained from eBay is used to provide marketplace research, listing analysis, and seller-performance insights.

MarketScope v1.4 uses read-only seller permissions and does not automatically change listing prices, send offers, or execute transactions.

## 4. Storage and Security

MarketScope is a desktop application. Seller OAuth refresh tokens are stored using the operating system's credential vault, and access tokens are maintained in application memory.

Seller reports are retrieved from eBay and displayed within the application. Market research history and application preferences may be stored locally according to application settings.

## 5. Data Sharing

MarketScope communicates with eBay's official APIs to provide its functionality.

The current application does not sell seller-account information to advertisers or data brokers.

## 6. Data Retention and Account Disconnection

Users can disconnect their seller account within MarketScope, which removes the locally stored OAuth refresh token.

Users can also revoke application access through their eBay account settings.

Locally stored research history and application data can be deleted from the device.

## 7. User Rights and Requests

Users may request information about the handling, correction, or deletion of personal information associated with the application by contacting the application operator.

## 8. Updates to This Policy

This policy may be updated when MarketScope introduces additional features or changes its data-handling practices.
